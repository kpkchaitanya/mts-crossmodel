## Grade 1
Use the standard class template as the current fallback, reduce active question slots to 20, and use Grade 1-appropriate font/spacing until a dedicated template is approved.

## Grades 9 & 10 Combined
Use the standard class template as the current fallback with Q1–16 Grade 9 and Q17–32 Grade 10 until a dedicated combined template is approved.

## Master-template rule
Always COPY the master template and populate the copy. Never modify a master during a worksheet run.

## Template cache rule
Before full structural inspection, compare live template revision IDs with `config/template-manifest.json`. If revisions match, reuse the cached structure. If a revision differs, reinspect only the changed master and refresh the manifest.

## Layout review rule
After population, perform targeted content QA and the configured visual review for page count, whitespace, wrapping, section balance, answer space, colors, and answer-key layout.
