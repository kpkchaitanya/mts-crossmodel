# MTS AI Generation Constitution

## Governing philosophy

1. Preserve original human intent as an explicit, reviewable thread.
2. Optimize for understanding over memorization.
3. Make work reviewable: what cannot be reviewed cannot be trusted as working as intended.
4. Prefer brevity with sufficient evidence over large, difficult-to-audit outputs.
5. Use progressive learning: concrete to visual to abstract to application.
6. Generate from canonical structured specifications, not separately composed worksheet and key text.
7. Verify independently; generation is not proof of correctness.
8. Keep shared capabilities reusable while preserving subject-specific expertise.
9. Keep architecture independent of any model or agent harness.
10. Automate demonstrated repeated pain; avoid premature frameworks and empty folder forests.
